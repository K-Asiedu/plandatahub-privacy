PlanDataHub ("we", "the app") is a field data collection and planning app for students and professionals, run from Kumasi, Ghana. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account details
Your name and email address, and your Google or Apple sign-in identifier if you use those options.
Location
Your GPS coordinates, only while you use the app, when you capture field data, submit forms or mark attendance in the learning hub.
Photos and files
Photos you take with the camera or choose from your library, and files you attach to forms and requests.
Content you create
Form responses, field points and sessions, requests, project submissions and messages inside the app.
Payments
Payments for consultancy jobs are handled by Paystack. We receive a payment reference and status, and consultants provide payout details so they can be paid.
Technical data
A push notification token, so we can send you notifications, and basic device information used to keep the app working.

Why we use it

To run your account, collect and store your field data, verify attendance, process consultancy jobs and payments, send notifications you have turned on, and keep the app secure.

Who processes it

Your data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (sign-in, database, notifications, security checks), Google Maps (maps), Apple and Google (sign-in) and Paystack (payments). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

Your data is visible to other people only where the feature requires it, for example a client and the consultant working on the same project.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app from the Profile screen, which removes your account and the data linked to it. You can also email us to ask for deletion or a copy of your data.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
